## Title

Quantum 2048: Merge Puzzle

## Short Description

Merge 2048 tiles in a quantum puzzle with Collapse choices.

## Full Description

Quantum 2048: Merge Puzzle is not just another 2048 clone. It turns the familiar merge puzzle into a quantum puzzle where unstable tiles can enter superposition, and the Collapse mechanic lets you spend energy to choose a tile's future.

Play Classic mode when you want clean 2048 number puzzle strategy. Switch to Quantum mode when you want a deeper brain game with particles, reactions, energy, superposition, and meaningful Collapse decisions. Some tiles can hold multiple possible values; tap them, stabilize one outcome, and keep the board alive.

Daily Challenge gives everyone the same UTC-based seed for the day, so your run is about planning rather than luck. Local achievements, statistics, and score history track your long-term mastery without requiring an account or internet connection.

Entanglement foundations are prepared for future quantum gameplay expansion. In this release, the public game focuses on Classic 2048, Quantum Collapse, daily challenges, achievements, and local progress.

Features:

- Classic 2048 merge gameplay
- Quantum puzzle mode with particles, energy, superposition, and Collapse
- Daily challenge with local score history
- Achievement progress bars
- Local statistics for Classic and Quantum
- Persian and English interface
- Offline-first design with local saves
