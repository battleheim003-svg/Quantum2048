## Title

Quantum 2048: Fusion

## Short Description

## Full Description

Quantum 2048: Fusion reimagines the familiar 2048 merge formula as a sharper quantum puzzle and brain game.

Play Classic mode when you want clean 2048 merge strategy. Switch to Quantum mode when you want unstable tiles, particle reactions, energy, and Collapse decisions. Some tiles can hold multiple possible values; tap them, spend energy, and choose the future before the board closes in.

Daily Challenge gives everyone the same UTC-based seed for the day, so your run is about planning rather than luck. Local achievements and statistics track your long-term mastery without requiring an account or internet connection.

Features:

- Classic 2048 merge gameplay
- Quantum puzzle mode with particles, energy, and Collapse
- Daily challenge with local score history
- Achievement progress bars
- Local statistics for Classic and Quantum
- Persian and English interface
- Offline-first design with local saves

Entanglement foundations are prepared behind a feature flag for future expansion, but the public gameplay remains stable and approachable in this release.
